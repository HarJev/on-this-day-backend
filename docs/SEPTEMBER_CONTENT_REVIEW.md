## Editorial Checks

- Each retained source was opened directly; search-only candidates were replaced
  where the source could not be retrieved. Claims are limited to the cited facts.
- Original summaries, descriptions and explanations; no copied article passages.
- Choice distractors are distinct and leave one correct answer.
- Existing question prompts checked to avoid exact/fact-equivalent duplication.
- Dates distinguish signing from ratification, proclamation from earlier federal
  independence, and a voyage's port departure from its earlier departure inland.
- New Zealand suffrage is parliamentary voting, not candidacy in the same year.
- The preliminary Emancipation Proclamation did not instantly abolish all slavery.
- Camp David agreements are distinguished from the 1979 peace treaty.
- Magellan did not personally complete the circumnavigation.
- No unverified or unlicensed images added. New featured entries intentionally
  use the supported image-free layout and have notification copy.
- Corrected existing smallpox wording to avoid a claim about all animal diseases;
  clarified the timezone of Armstrong's first step. IDs/types remain unchanged.
