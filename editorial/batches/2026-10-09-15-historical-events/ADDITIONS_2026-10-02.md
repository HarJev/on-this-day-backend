## Britannica day-page framing problems found

These were caught by checking article pages, and the drafts follow the article
or primary source:

- Oct 11: the day page calls Kathryn Sullivan "the first woman to walk in
  space". She was the first American woman; Svetlana Savitskaya was first (July
  1984), per NASA.
- Oct 12: Britannica's Bali article dates the bombings October 11 (UTC); the
  local date and time were October 12, about 11 p.m. (ABC Australia).
- Oct 9: the day page says Boulder (Hoover) Dam began generating on October 9,
  1936; a Bureau of Reclamation chronology (seen only in search results) lists
  other dates for the first generating units. Not confirmed, so dropped.
- Oct 15: the day page says the Antarctic Treaty conference concluded with
  signing on October 15, 1959; the treaty was signed on December 1, 1959. Not
  used.
- Oct 11: the day page says Pioneer 1 was launched "into lunar orbit"; the probe
  is generally described as falling short of the Moon. Not checked, not used.
