## New Questions

### 1. nigeria-capital-at-independence

Which city was Nigeria's capital when the country became independent in 1960?

A. Abuja

B. Lagos **(correct)**

C. Kano

D. Ibadan

**Explanation:** Lagos became the capital of independent Nigeria in 1960. Abuja replaced it as the federal capital in December 1991, and Lagos remains the country's largest city.

**Type:** multiple choice. **Difficulty:** medium. **Date:** 10-01.
**Related event:** `nigeria-independence-1960`.

Sources: [Encyclopaedia Britannica - Lagos](https://www.britannica.com/place/Lagos-Nigeria).

### 2. prc-proclamation-tiananmen

From which Beijing landmark did Mao Zedong proclaim the People's Republic of China in 1949?

A. The Temple of Heaven

B. The Summer Palace

C. Tiananmen Gate **(correct)**

D. The Drum Tower

**Explanation:** On October 1, 1949, Mao proclaimed the founding of the new government from the Tiananmen Gate Tower in Beijing.

**Type:** multiple choice. **Difficulty:** medium. **Date:** 10-01.
**Related event:** `peoples-republic-china-proclaimed-1949`.

Sources: [Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the PRC - Founding of New China](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/eng./zy/jj/zggcddwjw100ggs/jszgddzg/202406/t20240606_11377939.html).

### 3. snoopy-comic-strip

Snoopy is a character from which comic strip?

A. Garfield

B. Calvin and Hobbes

C. Blondie

D. Peanuts **(correct)**

**Explanation:** Snoopy belongs to Charles Schulz's Peanuts, which first appeared on October 2, 1950, in seven newspapers. His popularity grew in the 1960s as he became a more imaginative character.

**Type:** multiple choice. **Difficulty:** easy. **Date:** 10-02.
**Related event:** `peanuts-debuts-1950`.

Sources: [Charles M. Schulz Museum - Schulz biography](https://schulzmuseum.org/about-schulz/schulz-biography/).

### 4. mount-rushmore-not-carved

Which of these presidents is NOT carved on Mount Rushmore?

A. George Washington

B. Thomas Jefferson

C. Abraham Lincoln

D. Franklin D. Roosevelt **(correct)**

**Explanation:** The memorial shows George Washington, Thomas Jefferson, Theodore Roosevelt and Abraham Lincoln. Carving began on October 4, 1927, and Franklin D. Roosevelt is often confused with his cousin Theodore.

**Type:** multiple choice. **Difficulty:** medium. **Date:** 10-04.
**Related event:** `mount-rushmore-carving-begins-1927`.

Sources: [National Park Service - Mount Rushmore National Memorial](https://www.nps.gov/moru/index.htm); [National Park Service - Carving history](https://www.nps.gov/moru/learn/historyculture/carving-history.htm).

### 5. night-watch-painter

Which Dutch artist painted The Night Watch?

A. Johannes Vermeer

B. Rembrandt **(correct)**

C. Frans Hals

D. Vincent van Gogh

**Explanation:** Rembrandt painted The Night Watch, a commissioned group portrait of a militia company, around 1640-42. He died in Amsterdam on October 4, 1669.

**Type:** multiple choice. **Difficulty:** easy. **Date:** 10-04.
**Related event:** `rembrandt-dies-1669`.

Sources: [Encyclopaedia Britannica - Rembrandt](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Rembrandt-van-Rijn).

### 6. lepanto-wounded-writer

Which famous writer was wounded fighting at the Battle of Lepanto in 1571?

A. Miguel de Cervantes **(correct)**

B. William Shakespeare

C. Lope de Vega

D. Michel de Montaigne

**Explanation:** Cervantes, later the author of Don Quixote, sailed with the Holy League fleet and was among its wounded at Lepanto.

**Type:** multiple choice. **Difficulty:** medium. **Date:** 10-07.
**Related event:** `battle-of-lepanto-1571`.

Sources: [Encyclopaedia Britannica - Battle of Lepanto](https://www.britannica.com/event/Battle-of-Lepanto); [Encyclopaedia Britannica - Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Miguel-de-Cervantes).

### 7. opium-war-ceded-island

The settlement of the First Opium War, extended by the 1843 Treaty of the Bogue, made China cede which island to Britain?

A. Macau

B. Taiwan

C. Hainan

D. Hong Kong Island **(correct)**

**Explanation:** The 1842 Treaty of Nanjing required China to cede Hong Kong Island to Britain. The supplementary Treaty of the Bogue, signed October 8, 1843, added extraterritoriality and most-favoured-nation rights.

**Type:** multiple choice. **Difficulty:** medium. **Date:** 10-08.
**Related event:** `treaty-of-the-bogue-1843`.

Sources: [Encyclopaedia Britannica - Opium Wars](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Opium-Wars).
