### 5. exoplanet-meaning

What makes a planet an exoplanet?

A. It is outside our Solar System **(correct)**

B. It is made mainly of gas

C. It has a mainly icy surface

D. It orbits the Sun beyond Neptune

**Explanation:** An exoplanet is a planet beyond our Solar System, not a planet defined by its material. Many orbit other stars, while some wander without a host star.

**Difficulty:** easy. **Date:** 10-06.
**Related event:** `first-exoplanet-announced-1995`.

### 7. project-mercury-primary-goal

Which crewed-flight goal was central to NASA's Project Mercury?

A. Landing astronauts on the Moon

B. Sending astronauts past Mars

C. Sending astronauts into Earth orbit **(correct)**

D. Building a permanently crewed station

**Explanation:** Mercury aimed to put a crewed spacecraft into Earth orbit, study how a person could function in space, and return both astronaut and spacecraft safely. It was a step toward later, more ambitious human spaceflight.

**Difficulty:** easy. **Date:** 10-07.
**Related event:** `project-mercury-approved-1958`.

Sources: [NASA - Project Mercury objectives and guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/project-mercury/).

### 8. baseball-perfect-game-meaning

Over a full nine-inning baseball game, what distinguishes a perfect game?

A. Allowing no opposing batter to get a hit

B. Allowing no opposing batter to score a run

C. Allowing no opposing batter to hit a home run

D. Allowing no opposing batter to reach base **(correct)**

**Explanation:** A perfect game allows no opposing batter to reach base, even through a walk or fielding error. A no-hitter alone is not enough. Don Larsen achieved the feat in the 1956 World Series.

**Difficulty:** medium. **Date:** 10-08.
**Related event:** `don-larsen-perfect-game-1956`.

Sources: [National Baseball Hall of Fame - Baseball perfection](https://baseballhall.org/discover/lee-richmond-created-baseball-perfection); [National Baseball Hall of Fame - Don Larsen's perfect game](https://baseballhall.org/discover/inside-pitch/larsen-perfect-game).
